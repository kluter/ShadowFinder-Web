How ShadowFinder Web works
  1. Add a photo: drop it, paste it, or click Browse. If it carries an EXIF capture time, the date and time are filled in for you.
  2. Mark 3 points on the photo: the base of the object, its top, then the tip of the shadow. No photo? Type the Height and shadow Length instead.
  3. Enter the date and time the photo was taken, and pick UTC or Local.
  4. The band appears on the map by itself: every place where that shadow could fall at that moment.
  5. Narrow it down (optional): click + next to the tab for another observation of the same place at a different time. Where the bands overlap is the location. Two bands give a fix, a third resolves any ambiguity, so the tool caps it at three. Fixed webcams are ideal for this.
⚠ Shadow length is only accurate when the camera is roughly perpendicular to the shadow, so the shadow runs sideways across the frame. If it points toward or away from you it looks foreshortened, and the result can't be guaranteed accurate. The tool shows this angle and warns you.
